# Research & Engineering Portfolio

Personal research and engineering portfolio of **Juan Felipe Quintero**, showcasing academic background, experimental research experience and technical projects in scientific instrumentation, optical metrology and applied photonics.

**[View Live Portfolio](https://felipequi.github.io/research-engineering-portfolio/)**

## About

This portfolio documents my academic and professional development as an engineering graduate with a background in Engineering Physics and a French engineering double-degree program.

My technical interests and experience focus on:

- **Instrumentation & Metrology** — Experimental measurement systems, optical characterization and test benches.
- **Photonics & Optics** — Fluorescence spectroscopy, optical systems and photoluminescence.
- **Sensors & Imaging** — Optical detection, scientific cameras and image acquisition.
- **Materials Characterization** — Experimental methods, microscopy and device characterization.
- **Scientific Computing** — Python-based image processing, data analysis and visualization.

The portfolio is intended to support applications for R&D engineering positions, industrial research opportunities and PhD programs.

## Local Development

1. Clone the repository:

   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/felipequi/research-engineering-portfolio.git
   ```

2. Open the project in Visual Studio Code.

3. Open `index.html` using Live Server, or directly in a browser.

4. Modify the appropriate HTML or CSS files.

The website does not require package installation or a build step.

## Research Integrity & Publication Policy

This portfolio is intended to present technically accurate, publicly shareable descriptions of research and engineering experience.

- Confidential laboratory information and proprietary datasets are not published.
- Experimental results are not fabricated or exaggerated.
- Preliminary observations are distinguished from validated conclusions.
- Institutional figures and external resources require appropriate attribution and publication permissions.
- Illustrative figures should be identified as such when necessary.
- Research contributions should accurately reflect the work personally performed.
